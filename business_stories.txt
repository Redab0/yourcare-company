# KW Clean Business - Feature Stories

## Authentication & User Management
1. **User Registration**
   - As a new business owner, I want to register my cleaning business on the platform
   - As a manager, I want to be invited to join an existing business
   - As a worker, I want to be added to the system by my manager or business owner
   - Business criteria: Staff members must have appropriate role-based access

2. **User Authentication**
   - As a user, I want to securely log in to the business application
   - As a user, I want my session to persist until I explicitly log out
   - As a user, I want to recover my password if forgotten
   - Business criteria: Authentication must use JWT with refresh token mechanism

3. **User Profile Management**
   - As a business owner, I want to update my business profile information
   - As a manager or worker, I want to update my personal profile
   - As a business owner, I want to add/remove/update staff members
   - Business criteria: Staff profile changes should be logged for audit purposes

## Cleaning Request Management
4. **Cleaning Request Listing**
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to view all incoming cleaning requests
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to filter requests by type (deep cleaning vs. housekeeping)
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to sort requests by date, status, or location
   - Business criteria: List should refresh automatically or provide manual refresh capability

5. **Deep Cleaning Quote Management**
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to review deep cleaning requests that need quotes
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to submit price quotes for deep cleaning requests
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to set expiry dates for quotes
   - Business criteria: Quotes must include detailed descriptions and pricing breakdown

6. **Request Status Workflow**
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to accept house keeping requests directly
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to track when quotes are accepted by customers
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to mark jobs as in-progress when work begins
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to mark jobs as complete when finished
   - Business criteria: Status changes should create timestamps and log who made the change

## Scheduling & Assignment
7. **Job Scheduling**
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to see all scheduled cleaning jobs on a calendar
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to manage scheduling conflicts
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to set buffer times between jobs
   - Business criteria: Schedule should prevent double-booking staff

8. **Staff Assignment**
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to assign workers to specific jobs
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to create crews (teams of workers)
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to assign crews to larger jobs
   - Business criteria: Assignment should consider worker skills, location, and availability

9. **Work Order Management**
   - As a business owner or manager, I want to create detailed work orders for each job
   - As a worker, I want to see my assigned work orders
   - As a worker, I want to mark tasks as complete within a work order
   - Business criteria: Work orders should include customer requirements and special instructions

## Business Operations & Reporting
10. **Revenue Reporting**
    - As a business owner, I want to view monthly revenue reports
    - As a business owner, I want to see revenue breakdowns by service type
    - As a business owner, I want to track revenue trends over time
    - Business criteria: Reports should be exportable and include visualizations

11. **Job Analytics**
    - As a business owner, I want to analyze job completion rates
    - As a business owner, I want to track customer satisfaction
    - As a business owner, I want to identify top-performing services
    - Business criteria: Analytics should help identify business improvement opportunities

12. **Staff Performance Metrics**
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to track worker efficiency
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to monitor job completion times
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to review customer feedback by worker
    - Business criteria: Metrics should be fair and consider job complexity

## Customer Relationship Management
13. **Customer Communication**
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to message customers about their requests
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to send status updates to customers
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to request additional information when needed
    - Business criteria: Communications should be logged and associated with the relevant booking

14. **Customer History**
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to view a customer's service history
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to see notes from previous visits
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to offer loyalty incentives to repeat customers
    - Business criteria: History should include all interactions, not just completed jobs

## Mobile & Device Features
15. **Location-Based Services**
    - As a worker, I want navigation assistance to customer locations
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to optimize routes for multiple jobs
    - As a business owner or manager, I want to track workers' locations during job hours
    - Business criteria: Location tracking should respect privacy and only operate during work hours

16. **Offline Functionality**
    - As a worker, I want to access job details even with poor internet connection
    - As a worker, I want to mark tasks complete offline and sync when connection is restored
    - As a worker, I want to capture photos of completed work offline
    - Business criteria: Data integrity must be maintained during synchronization

## Security & Compliance
17. **Data Security**
    - As a business owner, I want customer data to be securely stored
    - As a business owner, I want to control who can access sensitive information
    - As a business owner, I want to comply with data protection regulations
    - Business criteria: Security measures should follow industry best practices and local regulations

18. **Audit Trails**
    - As a business owner, I want to track all changes made to bookings and customer data
    - As a business owner, I want to know who accessed customer information and when
    - As a business owner, I want to review logs in case of disputes
    - Business criteria: Audit trails should be tamper-proof and retained for compliance periods

## Integration & APIs
19. **Payment Processing**
    - As a business owner, I want to process payments for services
    - As a business owner, I want to send invoices to customers
    - As a business owner, I want to track payment status
    - Business criteria: Payment processing should support multiple methods and be PCI compliant

20. **Third-Party Integration**
    - As a business owner, I want to integrate with accounting software
    - As a business owner, I want to connect with inventory management systems
    - As a business owner, I want to sync with existing calendar systems
    - Business criteria: Integrations should use secure, standardized APIs

## Implementation Requirements
21. **API Requirements**
    - RESTful API design with proper HTTP method usage
    - Comprehensive error handling with appropriate status codes
    - Rate limiting to prevent abuse
    - Consistent response formatting

22. **Authentication Requirements**
    - JWT-based authentication with refresh token rotation
    - Role-based access control (RBAC)
    - Secure password storage with modern hashing
    - Protection against common authentication attacks (brute force, session hijacking)

23. **Data Storage Requirements**
    - Efficient schema design for cleaning service domain
    - Data validation at API and database levels
    - Appropriate indexing for performance
    - Backup and recovery procedures

24. **Performance Requirements**
    - API response times under 200ms for most operations
    - Support for at least 1000 concurrent users
    - Efficient handling of file uploads (photos, documents)
    - Pagination for all list endpoints

25. **Logging and Monitoring**
    - Structured logging for all API interactions
    - Performance monitoring for slow queries/operations
    - Error tracking with notification system
    - Regular security audit logging
